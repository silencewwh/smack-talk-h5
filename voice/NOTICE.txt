Enabling browser speech for the first time downloads the selected language model
(Chinese approximately 44 MB; English approximately 41 MB). Vosk caches extracted
model data in browser IndexedDB. Clearing browser storage requires another download.
Recording and recognition run locally in a WebAssembly worker. Microphone audio is
not uploaded to a speech service. No Google service or system speech service is used.
